02 · Lifecycle
Walk me through a typical job
From the moment an email or inquiry lands → to the job being closed and invoiced. Every stage becomes a ClickUp status. Edit names, add/remove stages, set who owns each step.
🎯 What to listen for
  • Stage names should feel natural to Lee/Zach. If they say "in the shop," use that — don't rename to "In Production"
  • Watch for parallel paths — wide format vs digital vs EDDM may diverge. If so, may need multiple Lists, not just statuses
  • "Awaiting approval" usually deserves its own stage — jobs can sit there for days
  • If Lee says "and then it's just done" → press: "Done how? Invoice sent? Or shipped? Or paid? Each one's a different signal"
  • Default scaffold: INQUIRY QUOTING APPROVED IN PRODUCTION READY DELIVERED INVOICED / CLOSED

03 · Data Capture
What info needs to be on the job at each stage?
For each stage, what data MUST exist before the job moves forward? Every field listed here becomes a custom field in ClickUp — and these are what the intake form auto-fills.
🎯 The "field elicitation" trick
  • Ask: "For Cady to quote this, what does she absolutely need to know?" — not "what custom fields do you want?"
  • If Lee says "the usual stuff" → press for specifics: "What's the first email back when stuff is missing?" = the required fields
  • Baseline from proposal: Quantity · Paper Type · Color Mode · Contact Email · Source · Assigned Rep · Job Status — already in the table below for stages where they typically apply
  • Mark a field "Required from intake" if the form should auto-prompt the client when missing
04 · Roles
Who sees what · who gets pinged · who owns what
Defines ClickUp permission tiers and notification rules. The matrix below produces the permissions plan in the build spec.
🎯 Notification hygiene
  • Notification fatigue kills adoption. The proposal explicitly says "alerted on assignments — not everything in the inbox"
  • For each person ask: "What do you want to know within 5 minutes?" vs "What can wait til morning?" vs "What never needs a ping?"
  • Lee likely wants exception-only pings (rush jobs, escalations). Zach wants ownership pings. Cady wants intake pings.
  • ClickUp permission tiers: ADMIN MEMBER LIMITED GUEST — Limited Members can't see Spaces marked private to them
05 · Email Routing
Where do incoming emails go?
The shared mailbox ORDER@EVERYTHINGPRINTING.COM is the entry point. Map known senders and keywords to where they should land. Start small — 2–3 of each — expand later.
🎯 Routing rule design
  • Senders are more reliable than keywords. Get 3–5 named senders first; keywords are fuzzier
  • For each sender, ask: "When you see an email from them, what's the FIRST thing you do?" = the routing rule
  • Keywords that are usually high-signal: "quote", "rush", "reorder", "PO", "proof"
  • Edge cases worth flagging early: (a) reply-all chains, (b) auto-replies from InfoFlow, (c) Loyalty Loop notifications

06 · Views
When [Role] logs in Monday morning, what do they want to see first?
Each role gets a default landing view in ClickUp. This drives the View configs per List — and the home dashboard for each user.
🎯 View design
  • The right question isn't "what view do you want?" — it's "when you sit down with coffee Monday at 8:30, what do you want to see?"
  • Zach's likely view: Board by status, filtered to "this week" — production sequencing
  • Cady's likely view: List filtered to "New / Quoting" — front desk triage
  • Lee's likely view: Workload view — capacity check across the team
  • ClickUp view types: LIST BOARD CALENDAR GANTT WORKLOAD TIMELINE TABLE
