Context

What this build is meant to solve

The discovery is centered on translating Everything Printing's real job lifecycle into a practical ClickUp structure. The goal is to create a system that handles intake, quoting, approvals, production handoff, readiness, invoicing, and internal visibility without overbuilding the first phase.

Today's scope is the operating structure: Spaces, Lists, statuses, custom fields, views, role permissions, and starter email routing rules. Future integrations and later-phase automations stay out of scope for this version.

Committed Scope

Phase 1 Deliverables

  • Workspace setup with 2 Spaces and roughly 5-7 Lists.
  • Company directory import from InfoFlow for about 1,000 client records.
  • Contact directory with linked records and baseline custom fields.
  • Shared mailbox connection for order@everythingprinting.com.
  • Starter email-flow automation using sender and keyword rules.
  • Intake form support for missing job details like quantity, paper type, and color mode.
  • InfoFlow BCC routing for estimates and invoices.

Workflow

Default Job Lifecycle

Inquiry Quoting Quote Sent Approved In Production Ready Delivered / Invoiced

The uploaded workshop doc frames these statuses as the starting scaffold. Each stage is intended to map to the data the team needs before a job can move forward.

Routing

Pre-Build Checklist

Client Inputs Needed

  • InfoFlow client CSV export.
  • Microsoft 365 admin or delegated mailbox access.
  • Five or more common email senders and typical message types.
  • Confirmed ClickUp subscription tier.
  • Examples of wide-format or EDDM jobs that may need their own workflow.
  • Three to five representative recent job emails for routing tests.
